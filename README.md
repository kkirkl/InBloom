After starting the app and acknowledging the privacy notice, the sign up with email button is the only one working at the moment. From there, entering any valid email and any matching passwords that are at least 6 characters long will progress you to the next screen, but nothing is actually being done with the email yet. Entering any 6-digit code will work on the next screen.

Once you have clicked through the next few screens, you will be at the main portion of the app. Two of the tabs, the garden and the profile, are fairly undeveloped and non-functional. The job tracker tab, however, allows you to add jobs, filter by completion status, check or uncheck application steps, and add or remove tasks. Unfortunately, I have not been consistent in disabling buttons that are not functional yet (although I plan to be better about that moving forward) so I can only advise to try everything.
